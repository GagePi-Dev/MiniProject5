Initiative Tracker is a small tool for running combat in Dungeons & Dragons and other tabletop games. It keeps your combatants in turn order and tracks their hit points so you can focus on the story.

How to Use

  1. Create an account. Register and log in so your encounters are saved to you.
  2. Create an encounter. Give it a name and an optional description, like "Goblin Ambush at the Bridge."
  3. Add combatants. Open the encounter and add each player, monster, or NPC with their initiative roll, HP, and armor class.
  4. Run the fight. Combatants are listed from highest to lowest initiative. Use the HP button to apply damage or healing as the battle goes on.

Combatant Types

Type Meaning
PC Player Character, controlled by one of your players.
Monster An enemy creature controlled by the DM.
NPC Non-Player Character, such as an ally, bystander, or neutral party.
