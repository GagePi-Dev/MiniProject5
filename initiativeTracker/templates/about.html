About Initiative Tracker

{% endblock %} {% block content %}

Initiative Tracker is a small tool for running combat in Dungeons & Dragons and other tabletop games. It keeps your combatants in turn order and tracks their hit points so you can focus on the story.

How to Use

  1. Create an account. Register and log in so your encounters are saved to you.
  2. Create an encounter. Give it a name and an optional description, like "Goblin Ambush at the Bridge."
  3. Add combatants. Open the encounter and add each player, monster, or NPC with their HP, armor class, and initiative modifier. You can type in an initiative roll or leave it blank to roll later.
  4. Roll for initiative. Click Roll Initiative to roll a d20 plus each combatant's modifier. Everyone is re-sorted from highest to lowest.
  5. Run the fight. Type an amount in a combatant's Adjust HP box, then click − to deal damage or + to heal. Use Edit to fix any of their details.

Combatant Types

Type Meaning
PC Player Character, controlled by one of your players.
Monster An enemy creature controlled by the DM.
NPC Non-Player Character, such as an ally, bystander, or neutral party.
